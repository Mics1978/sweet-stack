      How to play

      Move the falling trio of candies, tap ⟳ (or swipe up / tap the board) to cycle its order, and drop it. Line up 3+ of the same candy across, down or diagonally to burst them. Chains multiply your score.

      Touch: swipe left/right to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop. Keys: ← → move, ↑/Space rotate, ↓ soft drop, Enter hard drop, P pause.
