      How to play

      Move the falling trio of candies, tap the board (or the rotate button) to cycle its order, and drop it. Line up 3+ of the same candy across, down or diagonally to burst them. Chains multiply your score.

      One thumb: drag left/right and the candies follow your finger column by column. Tap to rotate. Flick down fast to slam it down; drag down slowly to ease it down. Keys: Left/Right move, Up or Space rotate, Down soft drop, Enter hard drop, P pause.
